Score Distribution
Model Details

Distribution from 50,000 Poisson simulations. Each team's goals modeled independently.

Poisson Goal Model
Each team's expected goals (xG) per game is estimated from their season GF/game, adjusted by the opponent's GA/game, then multiplied by the matchup factor (head-to-head advantage). Home ice adds +0.18 goals.

Win Probabilities
Win/loss/OT probabilities are computed from the Poisson PMF: P(home wins) = Σ P(H=h) × P(A<h) for all h. OT is modeled as 50/50 when within 0.5 expected goals.

Features Used
Goals For/Against per game · Power play % · Penalty kill % · Home ice advantage · Head-to-head record · Recent form weight (last 10 games).
